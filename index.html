2
Pattern Map Quiz
20 questions • no email required to start
The Experience
Dark, premium design. 20 questions across 4 sections: Nervous System State → Daily Patterns → Relationships → Identity & Self-Worth. Each answer maps to Fight/Flight/Freeze/Fawn. Takes ~3 minutes.
Why This Works
Zero friction to start (no email gate upfront). By question 10, they're emotionally invested. By question 20, they NEED their results. This is the psychological commitment that makes the email gate convert at 60-80%.
GHL: Quiz hosted as custom page or embedded HTML. File: FC-Quiz-Pattern-Map.html
3
Email Capture Gate
Name + email before results show
The Moment
"Your pattern has been mapped." — They see this AFTER answering all 20 questions. They've invested 3 minutes. Their curiosity is maxed. Name + email to unlock results.
Data Captured
First name, email, primary pattern type, all 4 scores. This means you can tag and segment by pattern — Controller gets different emails than Ghost.
GHL: Webhook fires on submit → creates contact → applies tag (quiz-completed + pattern-controller/runner/ghost/chameleon) → triggers automation

Results + Free Reset Delivery

"This quiz showed you what's running the show. The Day 1 Reset Protocol shows you how to interrupt it." → CTA to thedylanewing.com Free Reset page OR direct email delivery.
Email #1 (Immediate)
Auto-sent from GHL: "Your Pattern Map Results + Your Free Reset Protocol" — includes their result summary + Free Reset link/attachment. This is the lead magnet delivery.

5
Email Nurture Sequence
Pattern-specific • 5-7 emails over 7-10 days
Email 1 (Day 0)
Results + Free Reset delivery (already sent above)
Email 2 (Day 1)
"Here's what your pattern is costing you" — specific to their type. Controller = health/relationships. Runner = never finishing. Ghost = years lost. Chameleon = resentment.
Email 3 (Day 3)
Dylan's story — "I was The [Pattern] too" — near-death, came back, rebuilt everything differently. Personal, raw, your voice.
Email 4 (Day 5)
"Why the Reset alone isn't enough" — positions the Free Reset as the first step, Freedom Code as the full system. "The Reset cracks the door. Freedom Code walks you through it."
Email 5 (Day 7)
Freedom Code offer — full sales email. What's included, what changes, what it costs, how to enroll. Urgency if applicable (cohort, bonuses, limited spots).
Emails 6-7 (Days 8-10)
Objection handling + final push — FAQ, testimonials, "last chance" if using scarcity.
