## 1. The protocol, reconstructed

He calls it "the 10 Health Biblical Protocols." He never numbers all ten in one place in
the free content, but these are every step he gives publicly, in the order he repeats them.

1. **"Master's Cleanse" shot, every morning, empty stomach.** Half a key lime (he says a
   quarter to a whole, eat the skin and fiber too), 1 to 2 pinches of salt, 1/16 teaspoon
   cayenne, and "however much coconut or olive oil you can handle," in a shot glass, water
   optional. He says it does not break the fast. S4 S5 S6 S7 S9 S10
2. **Eat solids only between 10am and 4pm.** Fast 12 to 24 hours a day if you are a man,
   10 to 14 if you are a woman. Men should "work your way to every other day." S5 S6 S7 S8 S11
3. **Salt: 5 to 10 grams a day** of his "organic qualified" salt. A pinch or two on the
   tongue every morning plus in drinks and meals. S3 S4 S5
4. **Beef liver after every meal, ramped.** Quarter gram week one, half gram week two,
   three quarters gram week three, then "work your way up to two to three grams per day." S3 S4
5. **Goat milk daily.** "Anywhere from a quarter of a gallon to a whole gallon." Raw is
   best, powder if you can't get raw. S4 S5
6. **Psyllium husk and bentonite clay after every meal.** No doses given publicly. S4 S7
7. **Coffee rules.** No sugar. Add butter or coconut oil and a pinch or two of salt. Whole
   bean only. S3
8. **Squat, ground, and get sun first thing in the morning.** Start at 1 to 2 minutes,
   work up to 10. S3
9. **Teeth routine.** Coconut oil pulling 2 to 3 minutes in the evening, swish salt water,
   and eat the salt because "the magnesium in the salt helps to heal and regenerate your
   teeth." S6 He also sells a tooth powder and miswak sticks but gives no instructions. S1
10. **Food rules.** Only Leviticus 11 animals (beef, lamb, goat). No pork, no shellfish,
    no pizza. "Eat nothing sweet for 90 days... focus in on steak and eggs." Baking soda
    (not Arm & Hammer) before workouts. Distilled or spring water. S3 S5 S6 S7

**The 40-day fast.** It is one line in one video, and he walks it back in the same breath:

> "Fasting from solid food for 40 days is going to get rid of every disease in your body.
> If you were to eat no solids, but only liquids, smoothie diet, little key lime, salt, and
> water... Now, am I telling everybody, 'Hey, go fast for 40 days and 40 nights?' Not
> really, but at least start fasting 12 to 24 hours a day." S5

An Instagram caption indexed by Google says: "Avoid doing a 40 day fast... you need a
certain environment." S12 So the 40 days is liquids-only, not water-only, and he himself
tells followers not to do it. The daily protocol is the 10-to-4 window plus the morning shot.

**Where sources disagree.** He has videos titled "Why Salt is Bad" and "Why Fasting is Bad"
while salt and fasting are the core of everything he sells. Captions are off on the fasting
one, so I could not read what he means.

## 7. Red flags and contradictions

- **He sells every input to the protocol.** Salt, limes, cayenne, liver, goat milk,
  psyllium, clay, baking soda, tooth powder. All are commodity items available anywhere.
- **5 to 10 g of salt a day** is two to four times the WHO limit. The only "source" is an
  organization that does not exist by that name.
- **Cancer and medication claims.** He states cures for cancer, ED, herpes, and gout. Reviews
  on his own page describe people stopping insulin and "all prescription medications."
- **40-day liquid fast** is named as curing "every disease," then softened. No mention of
  electrolytes beyond salt, no refeeding plan, no list of who should not fast. The only
  safety note found: he does not recommend dry fasting for "9.9 out of 10 plus people." S4
- **Framing.** Illness is worms, demons, and "Beelzebub" in the bowel. The same videos cover
  reptilians, flat space, 2030 prep, and why men are gay. Health content is cut together with
  that material in every "Highlights" video.
- **Teeth.** "Regrow" claim rests on two of his own social posts. No photos over time, no
  dentist, no reviewer saying it happened to them.
- **Contradiction.** "Why Salt is Bad" and "Why Fasting is Bad" titles against a salt-and-
  fasting program.

## 9. Bottom line

The daily protocol is fully public: morning key lime, salt, cayenne, and oil shot; eat
between 10 and 4; 5 to 10 g salt; beef liver after meals; goat milk; psyllium and clay;
oil pull at night. The 40-day fast is a liquids-only idea he floats and then tells people
not to do. The $50 book would add a written meal guide and the numbered list. The $200 to
$499 kits are groceries plus the book. Nothing in the free or paid material offers
evidence for regrowing teeth beyond "do the protocol."
